# UGM Ilmu Komputer 2021 curriculum extraction

The revision-2023 FMIPA curriculum PDF is the controlling source for this dataset. DIKE UGM’s [Dokumen Akademik page](https://dcse.fmipa.ugm.ac.id/dokumen-akademik/) links the document as “Dokumen Kurikulum Program Sarjana 2021 Revisi 2023”; the linked source copy was downloaded from [UGM Simpan](https://simpan.ugm.ac.id/s/3LLHypTUeGoVDGH/download). SHA-256: `B4C07BD09D998527FDB8E680CD4A0C71AF54DE7CC1921C31CAC8DAC57C53E3E4` (17,739,212 bytes; 563 PDF pages). The departmental course page was also archived on 2026-10-22 at `output/sources/dcse-s1-ilkom-matakuliah-2026-10-22.html`; SHA-256: `2754FD7398012B60E056A1E90F7F734863D9F72EBBFDAEF071261F318AB47E61`.

The Ilkom chapter spans printed pp. 213–343, corresponding to physical PDF pp. 228–358. `output/data/ugm-ilkom-2021-pages.json` contains this page manifest and key page/table references. Complete extracted chapter text was kept locally for audit at `tmp/pdfs/ilkom-2021-full-chapter-pages.json`; the deliverables preserve the official PDF as source and structure the course tables, legends, selected rules, and source conflicts without copying its long narrative and syllabus text.

Table 3.26 is on printed pp. 256–258 / PDF pp. 271–273. Its extracted schedule has 68 coded rows: 53 actual course rows and 15 generic elective slots. The WP column is preserved verbatim. Two fully coded KKN rows marked `P` remain actual scheduled courses; only the incomplete `MII21-` rows are treated as elective slots. Table 3.27 electives are on printed pp. 259–263 / PDF pp. 274–278. It has 114 rows, including 111 complete course codes and three incomplete `MII21-38..` certification placeholders. Prerequisites are kept as raw strings, including asterisks, blanks, typos, and credit thresholds; no AND/OR or pass/fail logic is inferred.

The printed 104 mandatory SKS reconcile against the full schedule rows as follows: raw SKSW values sum to 125; choosing one of six 2-SKS religion alternatives removes 10 SKS; repeated term placements remove another 11 SKS (`MII21-3002` 2, `MII21-4001` 2, `MII21-4003` 1, `MII21-4002` 6). The resulting 104 matches Table 3.26. The printed elective total is 40, for 144 total. Semester subtotals printed in the table are 21, 22, 22, 22, 22, 21, 14, and 0, which sum to 144. Semester 8 still lists Seminar Skripsi (1) and Skripsi (6) beside its zero subtotal; this is preserved and called out as an inconsistency.

The departmental HTML page reports the older 98 mandatory + 46 elective SKS split. Its Bahasa Indonesia dan Etika Ilmiah row (MII21-1003, 3 SKS) differs from the revised PDF’s separate Bahasa Indonesia (UNU22-2013, 2 SKS) and Penulisan Akademik dan Etika (MII21-2010, 2 SKS). Its KKN row (UNU-4500, 3 SKS) differs from the revised PDF’s KKN PPM (4), Komunikasi Masyarakat (2), and Penerapan Manajemen Pengetahuan (2). The PDF also has an internal statement in §3.3.11.5 that the mandatory load is 97 SKS, conflicting with its 104-SKS summary and table; the dataset retains this conflict and uses the explicit Table 3.26 total.

The 2021 star convention is source-scoped: `*` means a prerequisite may be taken concurrently; an unmarked prerequisite must already have been taken through the final examination. These meanings are stated in both the 2021 departmental page and revision-2023 PDF. They are kept separate from 2026 prerequisite conventions. The certification placeholders and cross-references (`Table 3.47` in the Table 3.27 note, and `Table 3.35` in the Table 3.26 note) are retained without filling missing names or credits.

The structured JSONs record validation results: 131 sequential printed/PDF page pairs; source references on every course row; 104 + 40 = 144; semester subtotals sum to 144; the 125 − 10 − 11 SKSW reconciliation equals 104; all complete Table 3.27 codes are unique; and all three incomplete certification placeholders are retained.
